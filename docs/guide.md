# Getting started

1. Download the signed Android APK from [Releases](https://github.com/StevenShenPRC/UniEar/releases). Android 8.0 or later is required.
2. Pair and connect your headphones in Android Bluetooth settings. In UniEar, tap **Find connected headphones**, select the device, and tap **Connect** to open its control channel.
3. Once the control channel is ready, choose a listening mode or adjust the settings offered for your model.

## Quick controls

In **Settings**, enable the notification controls, Quick Settings tile, home-screen widget, or floating overlay separately. Tap the tile to cycle through supported modes. Long-press it to open UniEar. The expanded session notification shows model, battery and connection details.

## Automatic control and network rules

In **Settings → Headphone connection**, enable automatic control or leave it off and enable connection notifications. Auto-connect makes up to three attempts per Bluetooth connection. A connection offer lasts about 30 seconds and can be accepted or ignored. Android 16 can promote the notice to a Live Update, subject to system settings.

In **Settings → Network automation**, assign modes to individual Wi-Fi SSIDs or mobile data. Wi-Fi name matching requires precise location permission and system Location. Rules run during a UniEar control session.
